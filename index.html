Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does I ♥ PDFs process files without uploading them?

A: We utilize WebAssembly (Wasm) and client-side JavaScript APIs. When you select a PDF or image, the file stays stored in your browser's local sandbox memory. The computation takes place on your local CPU. Once processing is complete, a local download trigger is pulled, downloading the output directly from your browser memory.

Q: Is it safe to process confidential documents here?

A: Yes, it is 100% safe. Since files never leave your device, no third-party server can access, store, or view your data. This makes I ♥ PDFs the ideal tool suite for medical records, financial files, legal contracts, and personal IDs.

Q: Can I use I ♥ PDFs on my mobile phone?

A: Absolutely. Our responsive layout and local processing run flawlessly on mobile Safari (iOS) and Google Chrome (Android). You do not need to download any mobile applications or extensions.

Q: Why are there no file limits or watermarks?

A: Because we do not run expensive backend server infrastructure to process your files. Since your machine does the heavy lifting, our overhead cost is extremely low, allowing us to keep all features free, unlimited, and watermark-free.
